# Timeline — how the portal was built, session by session

The whole history on one page, oldest first. Dates are from the git log and
the notes of each session. The reasoning behind each step is in
[03-decisions.md](03-decisions.md); the requirement lists themselves in
[01-background.md](01-background.md). All dates are 2026.

| **9 Oct** | Two answers and a correction. The office: the **Special Budget approval upload stays optional** (its details box is still mandatory). And the owner asked why the portal said migrations 24-30 were missing from the hosted project when they had been run in the SQL editor — **it was these notes, not the portal**: the check recipe in `23-running-and-testing.md` listed markers only up to migration 25, so nothing past it could be verified and the "outstanding" sentence was copied forward each round. A read-only probe found **1-30 all applied**. Replaced the prose and the truncated query with **`npm run check:migrations`** (`scripts/check-migrations.mjs`): one marker per migration, `GET` of at most one row, exits 1 if any is missing, and **names 17 and 23** as the two it cannot see rather than guessing (function-only; PostgREST cannot read `pg_proc`, and calling them would be a write). |
| **9 Oct (afternoon)** | **The owner's own list: the portal stops talking.** The rule sharpens from 8 Oct's - out goes any sentence stating a rule the form already **enforces**, because a rule belongs on the Guidelines page, in the error when it fires, or in the control itself. Off the booking form: the infant note, "one of each", the parent-dependency hint, the per-room capacity notice (**both** places), the student banner "Double shared rooms will get first preference" (stripped from a **saved** Form Builder row too), the tariff paragraph, "At most 30 at a sitting", "The two must add up to N", the pets notice's second paragraph and six card descriptions - the first three constants **deleted**. Also **"What happens next"** off `/book`, the **"Copy to: Nobody"** row off the details card, eight page leads and the desk's section descriptions. The **availability panel redrawn**: a labelled strip of ruled figures with a 3px meter and **Full** in vermilion, a headline figure for one day, hour chips instead of a 24-row table, every cell keeping its sentence as an `aria-label`. The **privacy band** becomes three quiet links; **"Facing trouble booking?"** moves into the **portal footer** on every page (`MANAGER_HELP_LINE` deleted). **Booking History**: one row of selects, the fourteen date chips become one grouped `<select>`, the sixteen tick boxes move behind a disclosure that names what it holds - and **every role may export a PDF** (fixing `exportHistoryPdf`'s `defaultActor`, which would have filtered a requester's own report). A meal booking's **head count and diet split are typed steppers** again (reversing 1 Oct's dropdown), and a **dining booking is quoted meals only** - no room rate, no extra bed. 438 unit tests, 36 journeys. |

| **9 Oct (evening)** | **The offices' own debitable heads.** The office sent a spreadsheet instead of a list: one row per office mailbox, a column per head, "Y" where that office may charge it (kept as [offices-debitable-heads.csv](offices-debitable-heads.csv)). It is finer than the Settings console, which keys heads by **category** and so gave every office the same six - and it does not follow `office_class` either: the Director's Office spends the Institute Grant, a department office its department's budget, the Students Section the student and hostel funds, IAR the alumni fund, the Sports Officer the grant alone, the Institute Clinic Special Budget alone. `OFFICE_DEBIT_HEADS` (`lib/office-debit-heads.ts`) is the transcription, keyed by the **mailbox before the `@`** because that is the account that signs in, and `debitHeadsByType` applies it as a **ceiling over the Settings list** - an intersection, so neither list can widen the other and both the form and `createBooking` narrow from one computation. **Project Grant and Personal Funds are on no row**, which is the office saying an office spends neither. Mock Authentication puts the demo personas on other addresses, so a row may name **aliases**: the demo `official` ("Director's Office" on `admin@`) now offers Institute Grant and Special Budget and nothing else, and the demo department office Department Budget and Special Budget. The console cannot edit the table yet - deliberately, while the offices it keys on are not accounts. 451 unit tests. |
